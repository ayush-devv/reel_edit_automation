# Free / Low-Cost Royalty-Free Music and Indian Music Options for an Autonomous Reel Editor (paid client work, IG Reels / YT Shorts / TikTok, India)

Research date: 2026-11-19. "Accessed 2026-11-19" applies to every source unless a revision date is given. Quotes marked "verbatim" were taken from the page text downloaded with curl, not from AI summaries. Context: the pipeline renders one vertical video with music baked in and delivers it to a paying client who may post it on all three platforms.

## Q1. Licence per source: is commercial client work allowed? Attribution? Platform or ad restrictions? NC/SA/ND traps? Redistribution limits?

### Takeaway
Pixabay Music is the only large free source that explicitly allows client videos with no attribution. Incompetech (CC BY 4.0) is also safe if a visible credit is acceptable to the client. Bensound Free, Uppbeat Free, YouTube Audio Library and any CC-NC track (common on FMA, ccMixter and Jamendo Music) are unsuitable or risky for paid client reels. FreePD has shut down. Mixkit's music licence text could not be retrieved.

### Cited Findings

**Pixabay (Terms "Last updated: 20 October, 2026")**
- Verbatim licence grant for non-CC0 content: "an irrevocable, worldwide, perpetual (or as long as allowed by law), non-exclusive and royalty-free right to download, use, copy, modify or adapt the Content for commercial or non-commercial purposes" — [Pixabay Terms](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/)

This includes selling or distributing Content on a Standalone basis as an image, audio, video, NFT or other digital file (including through a stock media platform)…" The Terms define Standalone as "where no creative effort has been applied to the Content and it remains in substantially the same form". Combining audio with other media "is not Standalone use, so long as the combined effect is to make a 'new' creative work." — [Pixabay Terms](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/)
- Content with a "Published date" before 9 January 2019 is CC0 (public domain dedication). Later content is under the Pixabay Content License. — [Pixabay Terms §4](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/)
- Pixabay also bans political use. Verbatim: content may not be used "in a political context (such as the promotion, advertisement or endorsement of any party, candidate, or elected official…)". It also bans use alongside dating services, drug use and similar categories, and misleading endorsement. — [Pixabay Terms](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/)
- Verbatim FAQ: "Can I use Pixabay music in videos I sell or distribute? Yes. You may use Pixabay music in commercial video projects, including content you sell or distribute (such as client videos, promotional materials, or paid training content), as long as the music is part of a larger creative work and not distributed as a standalone file." — [Pixabay FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/)
- Pixabay says attribution "isn't required". Suggested credit: "by [Contributor] via Pixabay". Its FAQ also says Pixabay content can be used on social media platforms. — [Pixabay FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/)

### Inferences
- In a paid, multi-platform, no-credit-visible workflow, the defensible free sources are **Pixabay Music** (no credit needed, client use explicit) and **CC BY / CC0 tracks** (Incompetech, filtered FMA). For CC BY, the client must accept a credit line in the caption or description.
- Bensound Free cannot be used because client reels are revenue-generating, and its paid Business tier is the one meant for selling to multiple clients.
- A per-track licence field with an NC/ND reject rule is required for any CC-sourced track.

## Q2. Clauses on AI/ML use, automated tools, bulk download, building a local indexed library, or "competing service" bans

### Takeaway
Pixabay and Mixkit both expressly ban scraping, bots and bulk or mass downloading. Pixabay also bans compiling content into a "similar or competing service". Bensound bans its audio in "any tool that uses generative artificial intelligence". Suno bans using its outputs to train or power competing AI. CC BY / CC0 licences carry no such restrictions on the music itself.

### Cited Findings
- **Pixabay (Terms, 20 Oct 2026)**, verbatim:
  - "Data mining, extraction, scraping and the use of programs or robots for automatic data collection and/or extraction of digital data on the Service and/or the content available therein is strictly prohibited for all unauthorised purposes, including without limitation for machine learning purposes."
  - "Bulk, large-scale or systematic copying of Content is strictly prohibited unless explicit permission has been granted by us."
  - "use or compile any Content to replicate a similar or competing service to the Service, or copy the look or feel or the Service"
  - "access the Service for purposes of performance benchmarking or for building or marketing a competitive product"
  - Source: [Pixabay Terms §8](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/)
- **Mixkit (User Terms, "Last revised: October 2, 2025")**, verbatim prohibitions:
  - "use scripts or bots to mass download Items (this includes using any means whatsoever to scrape/download the entire library and/or database of Items)"
  - "use Mixkit or its related systems and networks, or any Item, to build a similar or competitive product or service"
  - "rent, license, sublicense, sell, resell or otherwise commercially exploit or make Mixkit or any Item available to any third party … including aggregate or collate an Item(s) and make available on a stock or inventory basis"
  - Source: [Mixkit Terms](https://mixkit.co/terms/)
- **Bensound (Terms)**, verbatim. Except as expressly permitted, you shall not:
  - "use the Audio Assets in applications or software that produces or generates videos, songs or any type of creative content (e.g. any tool that uses generative artificial intelligence);"
  - "use the Audio Assets and any derivative work containing the Audio Assets, in whole or in part, in any other stock product, library, collection, or database for distribution or resale;"
  - "use the Audio Assets for AI training…"
  - Source: [Bensound Terms §5](https://www.bensound.com/terms)
- **Suno (Terms, "Effective Date: September 3, 2026")**, verbatim. Users may not:
  - "use the Services (and any Output or Voice Model) to create, develop or improve any competing products or services or to power, enable or train other artificial intelligence and machine learning models, tools or technologies"
  - "engage in or use any data mining, robots, scraping, or similar data gathering or extraction methods"
  - Source: [Suno Terms](https://suno.com/terms)
- **ccMixter** says its Query API "is an open, publicly available interface that is available for public use, especially by 3rd party websites, mobile applications…". Rights in the music stay with each artist under the CC licence. — [ccMixter About](https://ccmixter.org/about)
- **CC BY 4.0** (Incompetech's licence) lets users "share — copy and redistribute the material in any medium or format and adapt … for any purpose, even commercially". FMA describes BY licences this way. — [FMA FAQ](https://freemusicarchive.org/faq)

### Inferences
- **Pixabay:** an automated pipeline that scrapes Pixabay music pages, or mirrors the catalogue into a local searchable library, conflicts with the anti-scraping and bulk-copying clauses. The "competing service" clause is a further risk if the tool exposes a browsable library to clients. A small, hand-curated set downloaded manually, then indexed internally and used only inside finished edits, is lower risk, but it is still "systematic" if large. Explicit permission from Pixabay would remove the ambiguity.
- **Bensound's AI clause** can be read two ways. It may ban only embedding the audio inside a generative tool (for example as a bundled asset library). Or it may cover any project produced by an AI editor. Either reading makes Bensound high-risk for this product.
- **CC BY / CC0 music** (Incompetech, FMA CC BY tracks, Pixabay pre-2019 CC0 items) is the only category where the music licence itself permits building a local redistributable index. The hosting site's own ToS may still restrict automated downloading. Incompetech's and FMA's site ToS were not checked.

### Gaps
- Incompetech site ToS on bulk or automated download: not retrieved.
- FMA site terms (the Terms page returned under 1 KB of text).
- Jamendo terms of use (empty response).
- Uppbeat terms on AI or automation (blocked).
- Mixkit's music-specific licence may contain additional AI clauses that were not seen.

## Q3. Content ID risk: which free sources' tracks get claimed on YouTube/Instagram, known incidents, and dispute process

### Takeaway
Content ID claims on legally used free music are a documented, expected risk on Pixabay and FMA, because contributors register their own tracks. Pixabay marks Content ID-registered tracks with a shield icon and issues certificates. YouTube Audio Library is the only source that YouTube itself says will not be claimed on YouTube. Bensound and Uppbeat offer paid channel safelisting. In-app libraries can be muted retroactively.

### Cited Findings
- **Pixabay FAQ**, verbatim: "some contributors or their distributors may choose to register their tracks with Content ID. This can result in automated claims even when the track is used legally." — [Pixabay FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/)
- **Pixabay mitigation**, verbatim: "Tracks displaying a shield icon are registered with Content ID and may trigger claims, which can usually be disputed using the Download certificate". Also: "License certificates are available for tracks registered with YouTube's Content ID. For other tracks, users should keep the download link, file name, and the Pixabay Content License summary as proof." — [Pixabay FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/)
- **Pixabay dispute steps** (YouTube Studio): Content → See Details → Dispute → "I have permission or a license to use the content". Include the track title, the Pixabay URL, the licence link and a usage explanation. Pixabay warns that a rejected appeal "could escalate to a copyright strike". — [Pixabay FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/)
- **Pixabay on other platforms**, verbatim: Instagram, Facebook and Pinterest "also use automated detection systems that may mute or block content… If needed, direct the platform to contact info@pixabay.com for verification". Canva audio that originated on Pixabay is covered by the same licence and the same claim risk. — [Pixabay FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/)
- A July 2025 Pixabay forum incident, where creators received false claims on a flute track via unauthorised redistribution, is reported only in a search summary. The primary post was not retrieved. — [search summary, Thematic vs Pixabay](https://hellothematic.com/thematic-vs-pixabay/) (competitor source)
- **FMA**, verbatim: "Some artists on the FMA have started using YouTube's Content ID system… FMA cannot address Content ID claims directly. When your video is flagged, reach out to the artists themselves." — [FMA FAQ](https://freemusicarchive.org/faq)
- **YouTube Audio Library:** YouTube says copyright-safe Audio Library tracks "won't be claimed by a rights holder through the Content ID system". YouTube "isn't responsible for 'royalty-free' music from other sources". — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882?hl=en)
- **Incompetech:** ISRCs are published so users can rebut false ownership claims. The FAQ links a dedicated "YouTube Content ID" article, which was not retrieved. — [Incompetech FAQ](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html)
- **Bensound:** channel clearing is offered per paid tier (1, 5, or unlimited "provided that you keep it reasonable"). Licensees may not register the music in Content ID, Rights Manager or the TikTok CML. — [Bensound Terms](https://www.bensound.com/terms)
- **Uppbeat:** "Channel Safelist" is a paid feature. Free accounts get per-track protection "With Uppbeat Credits". — [Uppbeat pricing (search snippet)](https://uppbeat.io/pricing); [makerstack](https://makerstack.co/reviews/uppbeat-review/)
- **TikTok CML**, verbatim: "If TikTok, at any time, loses the right … TikTok, in its sole discretion, may take down any videos … or mute the audio". — [TikTok CML User Terms](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/commercial-music-library-user-terms/en)
- **Indian labels' detection:** label content-ID systems reportedly "detect unlicensed use within hours, even if audio is speed-shifted, pitch-changed, or layered with voiceover". — [Tribune India / PTI, 29 Apr 2026](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/brands-make-a-beeline-for-music-licences-as-ipl-season-kicks-off/)
- Meta reportedly said more than 12 million Reels in India were muted or removed in 2024 for copyright violations. This is reported by exchange4media; the original Meta source was not retrieved. — [exchange4media, 18 Nov 2025](https://www.exchange4media.com/media-others-news/rs-900-cr-in-lost-royalties-the-hidden-cost-of-unlicensed-reel-audio-in-branded-content-149424.html)

### Inferences
- The pipeline should store, per track: source URL, download date, licence snapshot, contributor, and Pixabay's Content ID shield flag (plus the certificate where available).
- It should prefer non-shield tracks for clients posting to YouTube, and generate a dispute packet automatically.
- A Content ID claim on a client's channel is a client-relationship cost even when the dispute succeeds.

### Gaps
- No quantified claim rate per source was found. Vendor-blog statistics, such as "60% of disputes succeed", are unverified and were not used.
- Instagram/Meta's dispute process for royalty-free tracks: no primary page retrieved (Instagram help returned blocked text).

## Q4. API vs manual download; catalogue size and metadata

### Takeaway
None of the major free music sources offers a documented, sanctioned music-download API usable for commercial automated retrieval. Pixabay's API covers images and video only. YouTube Audio Library is Studio-only. ccMixter has an open query API. Jamendo has a developer API (not verified this session). Downloads should be manual or curated.

### Cited Findings
- **Pixabay API:** no source documents a music/audio endpoint. The API exposes `https://pixabay.com/api/` (images) and `https://pixabay.com/api/videos/`. — [Pixabay API docs](https://pixabay.com/api/docs/); [publicapi.dev listing](https://publicapi.dev/pixabay-api)
- Pixabay site-wide: "Over 6.4 million+ high quality stock images, videos and music". There is no music-only count. — [Pixabay Terms page footer](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/)
- **YouTube Audio Library:** "The Audio Library is found exclusively in YouTube Studio." — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882?hl=en)
- **ccMixter:** "The ccHost Query API is an open, publicly available interface that is available for public use". — [ccMixter About](https://ccmixter.org/about)
- **FMA:** the licence filter is available in the UI. — [FMA FAQ](https://freemusicarchive.org/faq). The FMA API page returned only a stub, so its status is unknown. — [FMA API](https://freemusicarchive.org/api)
- **Mixkit** bans "scripts or bots to mass download". — [Mixkit Terms](https://mixkit.co/terms/)
- **Hoopr** (Indian): "over 10,000+ premium tracks and sfx". Browse facets include genres such as "Indian Contemporary", "Indian Folk", "Indian Classical", "Indian Folk Fusion", "Devotional" and "Festival", and moods such as "Celebratory", "Ethnic" and "Spiritual". There is a "Sounds of India" section. — [Hoopr pricing page](https://hoopr.ai/pricing)
- **Hoopr Smash** reportedly has more than 18,500 Bollywood, regional and independent tracks. — [indiantelevision](https://indiantelevision.com/news-headline/licence-to-thrill-as-hoopr-smash-reshapes-indias-music-licensing-game)
- **Saregama's** catalogue is reported at about 130,000 songs, licensed to platforms rather than direct to creators. — [RouteNote, Triller deal](https://routenote.com/blog/indian-music-label-saregama-signs-a-global-music-licensing-deal-with-tiktok-competitor-triller/)

### Inferences
- The realistic path for the pipeline is a hand-curated local library of a few hundred tracks, tagged by humans or by the pipeline's own audio analysis (BPM, energy, mood). It should not depend on a live source API.
- Pixabay's lack of a music API, combined with its anti-scraping clause, means music retrieval cannot mirror the existing Pixabay video-API integration.

### Gaps
- Jamendo developer API terms (commercial reuse) were not checked.
- Per-source BPM/mood metadata availability was not verified, beyond Hoopr's genre and mood facets and FMA's licence filter.
- No catalogue counts were found for Pixabay Music, Mixkit, Uppbeat or Incompetech.

## Q5. Indian / Bollywood / regional music: legal options for business/brand accounts, costs, and how Indian freelancers handle it

### Takeaway
In-app libraries (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) are not cleared for branded content baked into an exported video. Indian labels actively sue brands. Real cases include Sony v Myntra (₹5 cr), Zee Music v Nykaa (₹1.75 cr) and Sun TV v CSK (₹1 cr). The legal routes for Indian-flavoured music are:
- Hoopr (production library; Smash for Bollywood and regional, self-serve per track or bundle; enterprise brand packages reportedly ₹6L–₹75L+ per year)
- Indian-genre tracks from royalty-free libraries
- Paid-tier AI generation (Beatoven, Suno Pro/Premier)

Free Bollywood music for brand reels does not exist legally.

### Cited Findings

**Platform in-app libraries**
- **TikTok CML (primary)**, verbatim:
  - "You may only post or share videos that include Commercial Sounds within TikTok… Commercial Uses outside of TikTok are not permitted and no rights are granted by TikTok for such uses."
  - "Commercial Sounds are the only sounds made available on TikTok for Commercial Uses."
  - Some "Premium" tracks "may be subject to an additional fee".
  - Business users grant TikTok the right to report usage data to rights holders.
  - Source: [TikTok CML User Terms](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/commercial-music-library-user-terms/en)
- **Meta Sound Collection:** an Instagram help snippet describes "over 14,000 songs and sounds which are entirely royalty free and safe to use in Reels and Instagram Stories" that "can be used for commercial purposes like ads". — [Instagram Help (search snippet)](https://help.instagram.com/ipad-app/402084904469945). Secondary sources say use is scoped to Meta's products only. — [Third Chair](https://usethirdchair.com/blog/meta-sound-collection-explained-for-music-licensing-teams). The primary Sound Collection terms were behind a login or blocked.
- Hoopr's CEO Gaurav Dagaonkar says "Most of the songs on social media platforms are only cleared for personal use." — [exchange4media, 12 May 2026](https://www.exchange4media.com/media-others-news/the-trending-song-in-a-branded-reel-whats-the-hidden-cost-154576.html). He also says Meta's policy limits library tracks to personal, non-commercial use. — [exchange4media, 18 Nov 2025](https://www.exchange4media.com/media-others-news/rs-900-cr-in-lost-royalties-the-hidden-cost-of-unlicensed-reel-audio-in-branded-content-149424.html) (Hoopr has a commercial interest)
- Secondary sources say business accounts on Instagram and TikTok see a restricted commercial library. Whether the restriction also covers organic posts is disputed across sources. — [Soundstripe](https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/why-can-i-only-use-commercial-sounds-on-tiktok); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-changes-rules-on-music-usage-by-businesses/577734/)

### Inferences
- **No in-app library fits a render-and-deliver workflow.** Meta's Sound Collection is reportedly Meta-only, and TikTok's CML is TikTok-only and in-app. Audio baked into one export for IG, YT and TikTok cannot legally rely on either.
- **Practical tiered offering for Hinglish clients:**
  1. Default: Indian-flavoured tracks from royalty-free libraries (Pixabay's Indian or Bollywood-style tags, Hoopr's catalogue on a paid plan).
  2. Upsell: a per-track Hoopr Smash licence for a specific Bollywood or regional song, priced through to the client.
  3. Optional: AI-generated desi beds on a paid Suno or Beatoven tier. The ownership warranty is weak, so they are best kept for background beds, not hero tracks.
- **Never use trending Bollywood songs** in client brand reels without a licence. Label detection reportedly survives pitch and speed shifts.
- **Suno caveat:** Suno disclaims that copyright vests in outputs. A client cannot then stop others copying the AI track, and third-party Content ID registration of similar output is possible.
