## What was built
| Area | What you get | Where |
|---|---|---|
| The Editor's "brain" | Reads all 313 knowledge nodes (index with line numbers) + a can/can't-do list of 221 capabilities, instead of a ~55-node shortlist | `knowledge.py`, `capability_status.py` |
| Pause tightening | Dead air inside a kept line becomes a jump cut (`max_pause_ms`, default 400 ms), including pauses hidden inside smeared ASR words (voice activity) | `compile.py` |
| Framing | Locked-off vs smooth speed-capped tracking per shot; blurred fill for wide two-shots; **crops to whoever is talking** (active speaker); over-the-shoulder shots frame the visible face; no stray frames at camera cuts | `framing.py`, `perception/speakers.py` |
| Motion graphics | Emoji pops (Noto SVG), keyword cards (pill/under/marker/box), lower thirds, self-drawing callouts, ₹/% counters, full-screen text cards, CTA cards, progress bar, logo — face-aware, safe-zone aware | `renderer/src/Graphics.tsx` |
| Footage effects | Grain, vignette, shake, RGB split, light leak, exposure pulse; transitions dip-to-white and glitch | `render.py` |
| Rhythm | Music starts so the most cuts land on beats; beat zoom pulses; freeze-frame holds; speed ramps | `compile.py`, `render.py` |
| Sound | **252 SFX** (47 synthesised by us + 205 Kenney CC0) and **33 music tracks** (Incompetech, CC BY — credits written automatically to `CREDITS.txt`) | `reel assets fetch`, `assets/` |
| Voice | DeepFilterNet3 noise suppression, strength picked from measured SNR, sample-exact | `enhance.py` |
| QA | Cut-point self-check (pops, flash frames, cuts inside words), caption gates that ignore graphics/titles, sustained reading speed, automatic calibration (`reel calibrate --auto`) | `cuts.py`, `qa.py`, `calibrate_auto.py` |
| Delivery | Cover frame / thumbnail (best eyes-open sharp face + hook title) in the package | `cover.py` |
| **Reel Studio** | Local dashboard: projects, new edit (drag & drop), live 3D pipeline, review with 3D phone, exploded 3D layers, revision chat, client notes, guided adjustments, deliver, libraries, brand kits, quality lab, settings/costs | `reel studio --open` → `studio/web`, `engine/src/reel/studio` |
