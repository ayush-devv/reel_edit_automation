# Conflict register: knowledge base v1

How conflicts were resolved, in priority order:

1. **ADR decisions win over drafts.** Where an accepted ADR sets a number or a rule, the node or capability is patched to match. The patched item carries `decided_by: [ADR-xxxx]`, and the draft figure is kept only as labelled reference.
2. **Classic film rules vs short-form grammar are scoped, not deleted.** Each rule gets the genres where it holds in `genres` (the `applies_to` edge) and the genres where it doesn't in `avoid_genres` (plus `avoid_when` text). The compiler filters on these per reel.
3. **Evidence hierarchy for everything else:** platform > study > practitioner > theory > single_opinion. When nodes are merged, the merged node takes the strongest evidence level and the highest confidence. On a parameter-key clash, the value from the stronger-evidence node is kept.

All edits live in `knowledge/tools/merge_plan.yaml` (`node_patches`, `cap_patches`, `merges`). The generated YAML is never hand-edited.

## B. Classic film grammar vs short-form grammar (scoped by genre)

| # | Conflict | Resolution (`genres` = applies_to, `avoid_genres`) | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-SCOPE-01 | **30° rule** (`principle.continuity.thirty_degree_rule`) vs **jump cuts** (`tech.cut.jump_cut_talking_head`). The draft tagged the 30° rule with exactly the jump-cut genres. | 30° rule applies to interview, brand_story, testimonial and product, and is avoided for talking_head, vlog, tutorial and podcast_clip. Jump cuts apply to talking_head, tutorial, podcast_clip and vlog, and are avoided for testimonial and brand_story (mask cuts there instead). On single-camera shots, a 110–125 % punch-in (`tech.transition.zoom_punch`) is the bridge. | Visible jump cuts are the native grammar of single-camera short form (practitioner, high confidence). The 30° rule still protects multi-angle and trust-led pieces. |
| C-SCOPE-02 | **"Cut long when unsure"** (Dmytryk) vs short-form compression (pause and filler removal, `anti.jumpcut.zero_gap` aside). | Applies at the **assembly stage in every genre** (`parameters.stage`). In the final cut it applies only to emotional holds in testimonial, interview and brand_story. Avoided for talking_head, tutorial, podcast_clip, vlog and montage final pacing, and for the first 3 s. | Reels invert the default. The ADR-0006 dead-air gate (> 1.5 s) also applies. |
| C-SCOPE-03 | **Never cut without a positive reason** vs a **pattern interrupt every 2–4 s**. | The interrupt now `requires` `principle.cut.positive_reason`. A text change, new point or new image counts as a reason; a random cut or effect does not. Emotional peaks are exempt. | Both are kept. The practitioner interval (low confidence) cannot override a theory principle that costs nothing to satisfy. |
| C-SCOPE-04 | **End right after the payoff** vs music **ring-out / backtimed ending**. | Backtime the music so its ending or final hit lands on the cut. Any ring-out tail is ≤ 0.5 s. | Satisfies both; also matches ADR-0022's clean-ending rule. |
| C-SCOPE-05 | **Native lo-fi look** (platform ad guidance) vs polished craft (grading, motion restraint). | Lo-fi applies to product, testimonial and vlog, and is avoided for brand_story and montage. | Platform evidence is about performance ads and organic content. Premium brand films and montages signal quality through craft. |
| C-SCOPE-06 | **Beat-synced cutting** vs **cutting on every beat** (`anti.music.cut_every_beat`). | Base cadence is every 2–4 beats. Every-beat cutting only in short bursts into a peak. Both draft pairs were merged. Speech-led reels cut on phrases, not beats. | The drafts agreed once merged; ADR-0022 snaps cuts to beats "where speech allows". |
